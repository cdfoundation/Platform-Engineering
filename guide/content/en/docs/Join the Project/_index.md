## What is the CDF Platform Engineering SIG?

The **Continuous Delivery Foundation (CDF) Platform Engineering Special Interest Group (SIG)** is focused on advancing platform engineering practices through open-source collaboration, practical guidance, and shared experiences.

The SIG is developing a **Platform Engineering Golden Paths Guide** to help organizations simplify software development, delivery, and operations through reusable workflows, standardized automation, and self-service developer platforms.

The guide will provide practical examples of how platform engineering teams can combine open-source tools to create golden paths that reduce complexity, improve developer productivity, and accelerate software delivery.

## Why This SIG Is Needed

Platform Engineering is becoming an important practice for organizations seeking to improve developer productivity, reduce operational complexity, and standardize software delivery.

However, many teams face similar challenges when building and maintaining internal developer platforms. They must evaluate a growing number of tools, integrate multiple technologies, and create repeatable workflows while meeting the needs of developers.

The CDF Platform Engineering SIG aims to address these challenges by sharing practical, open-source golden paths developed through community experience.

### 1. Reducing Developer Complexity

Modern software development requires developers to interact with infrastructure, CI/CD systems, cloud environments, configuration management, and operational tools.

Managing these technologies independently can create unnecessary complexity and slow down development.

Platform Engineering reduces this burden by providing standardized workflows, reusable templates, and self-service capabilities that allow developers to focus on building software rather than managing infrastructure.

Golden paths help organizations make common development tasks simpler, more consistent, and easier to maintain.

### 2. Improving Developer Experience

Developer productivity depends on having reliable tools, accessible documentation, and streamlined workflows.

Inconsistent development environments, manual provisioning, complicated deployment processes, and fragmented tooling can create friction and reduce productivity.

Internal developer platforms address these challenges by providing shared services and recommended workflows that simplify development and delivery.

The guide will help platform teams identify golden paths that improve onboarding, reduce repetitive tasks, and create better developer experiences.

### 3. Making Open-Source Platform Engineering More Accessible

The open-source ecosystem provides powerful tools for building internal developer platforms, automating software delivery, managing infrastructure, and monitoring applications.

However, determining which tools to use and how they work together can be challenging.

The SIG seeks to make platform engineering more accessible by documenting practical integration patterns, reference architectures, reusable templates, and lessons learned from real-world implementations.

By sharing community experiences, the guide will help organizations adopt open-source platform engineering practices without having to design every solution from scratch.

### 4. Connecting Open-Source Tools with Golden Paths

Organizations such as the Continuous Delivery Foundation (CDF), Cloud Native Computing Foundation (CNCF), and other open-source communities support projects that address different aspects of software development and operations.

The CDF Platform Engineering SIG focuses on connecting these capabilities through practical golden paths.

Rather than recommending a single platform or tool, the guide will demonstrate how open-source projects can work together to provide consistent development, delivery, and operational workflows.

This approach promotes interoperability, flexibility, and vendor-neutral platform architectures.

### 5. Supporting AI-Enabled Development

Artificial intelligence is introducing new opportunities for improving software development, automation, and platform operations.

AI coding assistants, automated testing, intelligent deployment workflows, and AI-assisted operations are becoming part of the modern developer experience.

Platform engineering teams need practical approaches for incorporating these capabilities into existing workflows while maintaining reliability, governance, and developer control.

The guide will encourage the community to share golden paths that support AI-enabled development and operations using open-source technologies.

## Goals and Objectives

### 1. Build a Community Golden Paths Guide

Develop a collection of practical, reusable golden paths that simplify common development, delivery, and operational tasks.

The guide will provide implementation examples, reference architectures, and open-source tooling recommendations that platform teams can adapt to their own environments.

### 2. Connect Open-Source Tools with Platform Workflows

Identify how open-source projects can be combined to deliver consistent platform engineering capabilities.

Document integration patterns, reusable templates, and automation approaches that demonstrate how tools work together across the software delivery lifecycle.

### 3. Improve Developer Experience and Productivity

Promote platform engineering practices that reduce developer cognitive load, simplify onboarding, improve self-service capabilities, and eliminate repetitive manual tasks.

Encourage practical approaches to measuring developer experience, platform adoption, and operational outcomes.

### 4. Share Community Knowledge and Experience

Create a collaborative resource where platform engineering practitioners can share successful implementations, lessons learned, architectural decisions, and practical recommendations.

The guide will evolve through community contributions and real-world experience.

### 5. Collaborate with Open-Source Communities

Work with CDF projects, CNCF communities, and other open-source initiatives to promote interoperability and share platform engineering best practices.

Encourage collaboration between platform engineers, software developers, operations teams, and open-source maintainers.

## Who Should Join?

Anyone interested in simplifying software development, delivery, and operations through platform engineering is encouraged to participate.

The SIG is particularly relevant to:

- Platform engineers building internal developer platforms
- DevOps engineers developing reusable CI/CD workflows
- Software developers seeking better self-service capabilities
- SRE teams improving application reliability and operations
- Infrastructure engineers automating environment provisioning
- Open-source maintainers developing platform components
- Engineering leaders improving developer productivity
- Organizations adopting golden paths and platform-as-a-product practices
- AI practitioners integrating intelligent automation into development workflows

Whether you are building your first internal developer platform or improving an established platform, your experience can help other teams learn and adopt practical solutions.

Visit the [CDF Platform Engineering GitHub Repository](https://github.com/cdfoundation/Platform-Engineering) to get involved.

## Help Build the Golden Paths Guide

Platform engineering is most effective when teams can learn from one another. Every organization approaches software delivery differently, but many face the same challenges around developer onboarding, automation, infrastructure provisioning, deployment, and operations. By sharing working golden paths, reference architectures, and practical experiences, the community can help other teams avoid common pitfalls and accelerate adoption.

# Get Informed

Successful platform engineering requires more than tools and technology. It requires practical knowledge, shared experience, and an understanding of modern software delivery practices. As organizations adopt internal developer platforms (IDPs), automated CI/CD pipelines, cloud-native infrastructure, and AI-assisted development, continuous learning becomes essential to improving developer productivity, operational efficiency, and software reliability.

The **CDF Platform Engineering Golden Paths Guide** is designed to help platform engineers, developers, DevOps teams, and open-source maintainers discover practical, open-source solutions for building and operating effective developer platforms. This Resources and Education chapter extends that mission by connecting practitioners with training, technical guidance, best practices, and community resources that support platform engineering and the adoption of reusable golden paths.

## Accessible Platform Engineering Education

Our goal is simple: Make platform engineering knowledge, practical guidance, and reusable solutions accessible to every software team, regardless of its size, budget, or technical expertise.

By combining education with the Guide's open-source tooling recommendations and community-contributed golden paths, we hope to help teams move beyond understanding platform engineering concepts to implementing practical, automated, and self-service developer experiences.

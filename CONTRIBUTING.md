# Contributing to CDF SIG Platform Engineering

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the CDF SIG Platform Engineering. This SIG is an open, community-driven group and welcomes contributions from practitioners at all experience levels.

## Ways to Contribute

### Join the Conversation

- Attend SIG meetings (schedule and connection details in the SIG repository)
- Join the [CDF Slack workspace](https://join.slack.com/t/cdeliveryfdn/shared_invite/zt-nwc0jjd0-G65oEpv5ynFfPD5oOX5Ogg) and find the SIG Platform Engineering channel *(channel name to be confirmed)*
- Subscribe to the SIG mailing list at [lists.cd.foundation](https://lists.cd.foundation) *(mailing list pending)*

### Propose or Discuss Topics

- Open a GitHub issue to propose a new topic, raise a question, or identify a gap in existing content
- Add agenda items to upcoming meeting notes
- Share experience reports, case studies, or tooling evaluations relevant to platform engineering

### Contribute Content

- Review open pull requests and provide feedback
- Draft new sections for the Platform Engineering Guide
- Contribute reference architectures, diagrams, or golden path templates
- Improve existing documentation for clarity, accuracy, or completeness
- Add yourself to the Members list in [README.md](README.md) once you've attended a meeting

### Present to the SIG

- Give a lightning talk or full presentation on a platform engineering topic at a SIG meeting
- Share how your organization uses CDF projects in your internal developer platform
- Demonstrate tooling integrations or proof-of-concept implementations

## Contribution Process

1. **Open an issue** to discuss your proposed contribution before writing significant content. This avoids duplicate effort and ensures alignment with the SIG roadmap.
2. **Fork the repository** and create a branch for your changes.
3. **Submit a pull request** with a clear description of what you've added or changed and why.
4. **Request a review** from a SIG maintainer. At least one maintainer approval is required to merge.
5. **Respond to feedback** during the review process.

## Content Guidelines

- All content must remain **vendor-neutral**. Do not promote specific commercial products. Open-source tooling should be referenced by project name and linked to the project's official repository or website.
- Write for **practitioners**. Assume your reader is a platform engineer or DevOps practitioner—explain the *why* behind recommendations, not just the *what*.
- Use **plain, clear language**. Avoid jargon where simpler terms work equally well.
- Include **real examples** where possible. Reference architectures, workflow diagrams, and code snippets make guidance actionable.
- Attribute external sources properly. If referencing frameworks (DORA, NIST, CNCF), link to the original source.

## Code of Conduct

All contributors are expected to follow the [CDF Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). The SIG is committed to maintaining a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful community.

## Questions

If you have questions about contributing, open an issue in this repository or reach out on the CDF Slack.

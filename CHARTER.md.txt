Platform Engineering SIG Charter
Continuous Delivery Foundation (CDF) — Special Interest Group Status: Draft v0.1 — for review by initial members and the CDF TOC Repository: cdfoundation/Platform-Engineering
1. Mission
The Platform Engineering SIG exists to advance the practice of platform engineering within the CI/CD ecosystem by building, operating, and sharing knowledge about internal developer platforms (IDPs) that let application teams ship software safely and quickly through self-service, golden paths, and paved roads. The SIG approaches this work with a security-first mindset, treating secure-by-default design as a baseline requirement of any platform rather than an afterthought, and actively leverages the latest AI technology to accelerate platform engineering practice from AI-assisted platform tooling and automation to AI-accelerated development of golden paths and self-service workflows.
2. Scope
In scope:


* Internal developer platform (IDP) design patterns and reference architectures, built to a security-first standard (secure defaults, least privilege, and supply-chain integrity as baseline design requirements)
* Golden paths / paved roads for build, test, deploy, and release workflows
* Developer self-service tooling and platform-as-a-product practices
* Developer experience (DX) and platform adoption metrics
* Applying AI technology to accelerate platform engineering 
   * AI-assisted tooling, automation, and AI-accelerated development of golden paths, self-service workflows, and platform operations
* Mapping open-source platform engineering tools to CDF and adjacent (CNCF, OpenSSF) projects
* Best practices, reference implementations, and educational content for platform teams


Out of scope:


* Vendor or product endorsements
* Duplicating the mandate of other CDF SIGs (e.g., security guidance owned by the CI/CD Cybersecurity initiative, interoperability standards owned by the Interoperability SIG) — the SIG coordinates with those groups rather than replacing them
* Formal standards-setting (may be proposed later as a graduation path, per the TOC's project lifecycle)
3. Goals and Deliverables
* A public reference guide / knowledge base for platform engineering practices, published via this repository
* Curated mapping of open-source platform engineering tooling relevant to Platform Engineering
* Regular case studies or talks from member organizations running internal platforms
* Quarterly progress updates to the CDF TOC mailing list, per TOC requirements
4. Governance
TOC Sponsor
A CDF TOC member sponsors the SIG, monitors its progress, and is the escalation point for resourcing or attendance issues. Sponsor: Tracy Ragan (DeployHub)
Chairs
The SIG is led by one or more chairs who set the meeting agenda, drive the roadmap, and represent the SIG to the TOC. Per CDF policy, chairs rotate at least bi-annually to ensure neutral leadership. Chair: Jennifer Mulford, Senior Platform Security Engineer (Okta)
Decision Making
The SIG operates by lazy consensus among active members. Where consensus cannot be reached, the chair(s) make the final call, escalating to the TOC sponsor if needed. Major changes to this charter require majority agreement among active members and sign-off from the TOC sponsor.
5. Membership
Membership is open to any individual or organization interested in the SIG's mission — no CDF membership is required to participate. Per CDF SIG policy, the initial proposal must show participation from at least three different companies.
